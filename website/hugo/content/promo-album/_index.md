Here are the participation conditions:

- The track has to be made with ChipNomad. No limitations on the used features.
- Only original music. No covers, please.
- Up to two tracks from an author.
- The track can be previously released, it doesn't have to be made specially for this album.
- You retain all rights to your music. You can release or use it anywhere without any limitations.

What rights I (*Megus*) gain:

- Non-exclusive rights to release tracks on Bandcamp and Subvert in the compilation album.
- Include project files in the ChipNomad distribution bundle.
- Use tracks in promo materials.

I will sign an agreement with each author.

The compilation album will be released on Bandcamp and Subvert with "pay what you want" price. I won't release it on streaming platforms. All revenue from the promo album will go to support the development.
